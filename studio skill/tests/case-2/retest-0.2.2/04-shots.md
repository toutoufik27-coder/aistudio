# Shots: The Dry Well

## Direction summary

**The idea.** The film is a measurement. Above ground the camera never moves; below ground it only goes down. The one upward move in the whole film is S07-02, when the rope brings the red rag up dry, and that is the shot where the village learns Anders was wrong. Red exists only on the rag, so when Ilse closes her hand on it in S07-07 the colour leaves the film.

**Scene purposes** (what changes for the audience):

- S01: we learn the village has one attempt, that Anders failed in public once, and we watch the vote leave Ilse alone with a number.
- S02: we see what her dig will be paid for with (his workshop, board by board), and she stops being alone.
- S03: the shaft goes deeper than Anders left it, and fourteen becomes a red mark we can watch for.
- S04: the number is reached and it is dry; our trust in the notebook breaks at the same moment hers does, and she sends away her help.
- S05: she digs on with no number to dig toward, and finds water two metres below the one he wrote.
- S06: relief, and the news gets out of the yard.
- S07: the village sees by measure that Anders was wrong; Ilse removes the measure; nobody says it.

**Three shots that show the approach:**

- S04-04: the notebook page and the dry floor in one frame, the drawn wavy line lying next to the place where the water is not. No cut between the claim and the fact.
- S05-03: the whole sixteen metres in one static frame, Anders's white numbers standing in the dark, a small silhouette climbing past every one of them for a single bucket.
- S07-02: the same framing as S05-05, where the rag went down. Now it comes up, and the camera goes up with it.

**Count and runtime.** 45 shots, 184.0 s against a 180 s target (+2.2 percent, inside the 5 percent limit). They are built from 23 setups, tagged in Production notes. Three of them (`SHAFT-CUT`, `YARD-WIDE`, `YARD-WINDLASS`) carry 19 of the 45 shots.

**Runtime moved between scenes.** S07 runs 32.5 s against the screenplay's 0:25 and S01 runs 32.5 s against 0:30; both carry dialogue and beats that could not be shortened without cutting text. S02, S03, S04 and S06 each give back one to two seconds.

**Cut or merged, with reasons:**

- S02, "looks down the ladder, then at the windlass handle" and Jonas's line: merged into S02-04. One head turn does the job of two inserts.
- S03, a separate shot of the bucket rising after the two tugs: merged into S03-03. S03-01 has already shown the way up.
- S04, a separate insert of the notebook page: merged into S04-04. The page was shown in S01-06; a second insert would repeat it, and holding page and floor together is the point.
- S05, the dig-fill-climb-haul-empty-descend cycle: reduced to the climb (S05-03) and the haul (S05-04). S03-03 taught the cycle; S05 only needs to show that she now does both ends of it.
- S05, "Again": no second pass of the cycle. S05-05 (the rag going under) carries "again" and "deeper" in one shot.
- S06, an insert of the wet rope for Jonas's look: cut. It would spend S07-03's reveal a scene early. The dark rope sits in the depth of S06-03 instead.
- S07, "Ruth watches her do it": no separate reaction shot. Ruth is held in the background of S07-07, not moving.
- S07, Ruth to the fence, the first villager through the gate, and Ilse sitting down: merged into S07-08. One frame holds the water, the posts and the two women.

## Known weaknesses

- **The fourteenth knot.** S03-05 cannot show that the knot she ties the rag on is the fourteenth without a count nobody speaks. The link between rag and number is made one scene later, in S04-03, by the "14" board, the rag at the lip and Jonas's line. A viewer may spend S03-05 not knowing what the rag means.
- **S05-02 adds a gesture.** The screenplay says she does not chalk a number. The shot shows the chalk raised and put away, so that an absence can be seen. If the user reads this as more hesitation than the scene intends, the alternative is to hold on the blank wood under the "14" for the same 3.5 s with no hand.
- **S07 is long.** 32.5 s against the screenplay's 0:25. The eight shots each carry a beat or a line from the text. The place to recover time, if an animatic shows the ending dragging, is S07-01 and S07-06, not S07-08.
- **45 shots in 10 days.** Tight for one person. 23 setups is more than two a day; it is workable only because three setups carry 19 shots and three rigs do most of the acting. If the schedule slips, the single-use setups in S01 (`SQ-FLAG`, `SQ-HIGH`) are the first to merge. The costly ones are S01-08 (twelve staggered arms), S06-02 (goat walk) and S07-08 (crowd movement).
- **Skin and ground share K2.** By day, hands and heads are kept against sky, timber or clothing. S02-03 and S06-02 are the frames where a hand is most likely to be lost against the ground.
- **Carried from the screenplay:** the council's failed dig is still shown only by Ruth's sleeves and the empty buckets in S07-01.
