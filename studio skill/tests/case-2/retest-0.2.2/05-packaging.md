## Step 1: The promise

- What the viewer gets: three quiet minutes in which a widow stakes her dead husband's name on one number in his notebook, and digs to find out if he was right.
- The most intriguing true thing: the village voted against her, so she takes his workshop apart board by board to shore the shaft (S02, S03).

The Dry Well is a three-minute 2D animated short. This story is fiction. The village, the people and the events are invented.
