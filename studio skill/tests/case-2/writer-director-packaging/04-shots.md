# Shots: The Dry Well

## Direction summary

**Approach.** The film is a measurement. On the surface the camera is side-on and never moves. In the shaft the earth is cut away like a diagram, one metre is always the same number of pixels, and the camera moves only up or down, only to count. Faces have no features, so every beat is carried by a hand, a posture, a number or the rope. Red is used on one object.

**Scene purposes** (what changes for the audience by the end)

| Scene | Purpose |
|---|---|
| S01 | We learn the village has one attempt, that it goes to the old well, and that Anders's earlier failure is the reason Ilse loses the vote. |
| S02 | We see what Ilse is willing to spend (his workshop) and that she now has one helper. |
| S03 | We learn how the depth is counted, and that fourteen is now a visible mark both of them are waiting for. |
| S04 | We learn the notebook is wrong, and watch Ilse choose to be alone with that. |
| S05 | Tension turns to release: there is water, and we see exactly how far below his number it is. |
| S06 | We know before the village does, and we see that the village is about to. |
| S07 | Held. Intended purpose: Ruth, and through her the village, learns the water is not where Anders said; Ilse removes the evidence and the question stays open. |

**Three sample shots**

- **S03-02**: the camera follows the bucket down the cross-section past Anders's painted numbers, 1 to 11, and stops where the numbers stop. It teaches the audience to read depth before anything depends on it.
- **S04-04**: straight down the shaft: the notebook page held over the floor, so the drawn wavy line sits on top of the cracked clay. The page and the fact are in one frame and they disagree.
- **S05-06**: from her boots in the water the camera rises two metres, past two blank boards, to the chalked "14" at the edge of the lantern light. The only camera move in the scene, and it is the measurement the whole film was counting towards.

**Count and runtime.** 32 shots across S01 to S06, 155 s. S07 (25 s in the screenplay's estimate) is held, see "Returned to Writer". With S07 at its estimate the film is 180 s against a 180 s target.

**Cut or merged**

- S01: the mud landing on the flagstones is not given its own insert; it lands inside the wide S01-02. A separate close-up would repeat what the wide already shows.
- S02: the look through the gap (workbench, tools, empty hook) is merged into the plank coming off, S02-01. The gap opening is the reveal.
- S03: digging, hauling, shoring and chalking "12" are one locked frame with time cuts, S03-03, not five shots.
- S04: no second insert of the notebook page. The audience read it in S01-05; here it is seen only against the floor.
- S05: the dig-climb-haul-empty-descend cycle is one extreme wide with time cuts, S05-03. Following her up the ladder in closer shots would hide the distance, which is the point.
- S06: Jonas's three looks (goat, wet rope, Ilse) are three head turns in one shot, not three point-of-view inserts.

## Returned to Writer

1. **S07, the rope coming up (blocking).** The rag is tied at the fourteenth knot from the bucket (S03), and it sits at the lip when the bucket rests at fourteen metres (S04). With the bucket at the water, sixteen metres down, the rag is therefore two knots below the lip (as S05 shows) and fourteen knots above the bucket. Hauled up as S07 describes, the rag would come out of the shaft almost at once, and the wet rope would follow fourteen knots later, not two. The line "the red rag rises out of the shaft, dry... One more knot. Another. Then the rope comes up dark and dripping" cannot be drawn as written without contradicting S03 to S05, and in a cross-section film where every metre is to scale the audience will see it. What the rope does show truthfully is on the way down: the rag passes the lip and two more knots follow it before the bucket lands. How Ruth comes to read the two knots is a story decision, so S07 has no shots yet. The rest of S07 (the two exchanges, Ilse untying the rag, the line of buckets past the bare posts) has no problem.
2. **P07 to P10 are not in the facts list (not blocking).** See 03-bible.md.

## Known weaknesses

- The runtime check fails until S07 is designed.
- S05-03 carries the cost of the night in 8 seconds with a figure 93 px tall. If it does not read in the animatic, it needs a closer second frame and the time has to come from S05-01.
- S04-04 depends on the audience remembering the page from S01-05, two minutes earlier, for 2.5 seconds.
- With no faces, S04-05 rests entirely on how long Jonas's head stays at the rim. The holds are guesses until there is sound.
- Asset load for one person in 10 days: 4 rigs, a crowd component, 3 locations with state parameters, 10 props, two walk or run cycles. The goat rig is the first thing to simplify if the schedule slips.
